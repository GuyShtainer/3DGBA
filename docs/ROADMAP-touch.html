3 · The order to finish in

TIER 0

The harness freeze

blocks everything below

With a debugger attached the emulator freezes ~20 seconds into a game session. The decisive test: the older binary freezes identically, so it isn't a code regression. Until this is fixed, nothing new can be emulator-proven — which is why Waterfall and Strength are stuck. This is the single highest-value thing to fix.

TIER 1

Finish the HMs

nearly done, high daily value

Run the Waterfall ride on the 8-tile Ever Grande fall — the only site that measures whether one interaction carries you the whole column. Measure Strength's safety property. Then give Dive an executor: it needs its own sequencer (own tile, no facing step, B to surface, map change as the success gate).

TIER 2

Screens you touch constantly

best value per hour

Summary pages 3–4, Mart buy, Bag item sub-menu, Player PC, Mailbox, Move relearner, Mail compose. Most are list-family variants, and the discovery probe means each is roughly one harvested screen id away with no new addresses. This is the cheapest bulk progress available.

TIER 3

Pokédex

self-contained, big surface

Emerald's list, entry, size and search UI; all of FireRed's dex including its grid TOC. Large but isolated — nothing else depends on it.

TIER 4

Word-grid text entry

new family, not the keyboard

Easy Chat, Walda's phrase, Dewford trends. These pick words from a grid, so the letter-keyboard driver doesn't transfer — check what actually carries over before writing code.

TIER 5

Contests, Frontier, minigames

deep, gated, rarely used

Contest appeals and condition screens; Frontier Pass, Factory swap, Pyramid bag, Dome tree; slots, roulette, Teachy TV, Fame Checker. Several are gated behind long in-game progression, which is why they keep getting deferred — that's a budget problem, not a difficulty one.

TIER 6

Secret Base decoration

the most touch-shaped thing left

Genuine drag-and-place. Deliberately last because it needs a secret base to exist before any of it can be proven — but it's the screen that will feel best.

4 · Only you can test this

Every result above is Azahar. The emulator cannot speak for the physical panels, the real digitiser, or two saturated cores at 804 MHz. The .cia is current as of 06:20 today and includes everything.

5 · Waiting on your decision

Traversal ships OFF. smartTraverse = 0, so Surf, Cut, Rock Smash and Waterfall do nothing on a fresh install. The reasoning was that a feature which walks your character around and answers the game's own yes/no prompts shouldn't switch itself on. You asked for the feature specifically — so: default On, On-without-cross-map-excursions, or leave it Off and opt in?

Where should effort go? The tier order above is my recommendation, but if you'd rather have the Pokédex before the everyday menus, or want decoration placement early because it's the most fun, say so and I'll reorder.

6 · The honest caveat

The plan document's own status column has drifted — it still lists the naming keyboard and PC storage as outstanding when both have been proven for days. Lanes updated their own rows and not others. The numbers on this page come from the lane reports and commits, not from that column, which is why I could not quote you a clean "N screens left" earlier. Fixing that bookkeeping is a small job and worth doing before the next push.

One pattern worth carrying forward: eight times this project asserted something was unreachable or impossible and was wrong — a credits sequence that replays on every league clear, a machine that turned out to be one staircase away, an evolution trigger sitting in a PC box nobody decoded. Positive claims were always earning live proof; negative ones were being reasoned once and inherited. Those are now checked as hard as the claims.
